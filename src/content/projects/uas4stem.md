*Demo of autonomous landing, payload pickup, and takeoff*

Implemented an autonomous navigation system in Python using MAVLink to communicate with our custom-built drone.
I also built computer vision pipelines for target identification using DepthAI and OpenCV, being able to detect
items of interest from 40m in the air and pinpoint their precise location for autonomous landings. We were able to
secure 2nd place in the 2024 UAS4STEM competition.
